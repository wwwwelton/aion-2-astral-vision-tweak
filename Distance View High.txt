Increases how far the game draws and keeps maximum detail. Marked, these lines override the in-game sliders.
Players, mobs, objects, and full-resolution textures remain loaded and visible across the entire screen.

View distance (players, mobs, objects, everything with a MaxDrawDistance):
r.ViewDistanceScale=2.5

Players and mobs (skeletal meshes - visible at maximum distance):
r.SkeletalMeshLODBias=0
r.SkeletalMeshLODRadiusScale=2.5

Objects (static and Nanite meshes - maximum detail at distance):
r.StaticMeshLODDistanceScale=0.25
r.Nanite.MaxPixelsPerEdge=1.0

Foliage and grass (drawn to the horizon):
foliage.LODDistanceScale=2.0
foliage.CullDistanceScale=2.5
foliage.MinLOD=0
foliage.OverestimateLOD=1
grass.CullDistanceScale=2.5

Terrain:
r.LandscapeLODDistributionScale=2.0
r.LandscapeLOD0DistributionScale=2.0

Shadows and lighting at distance:
r.Shadow.DistanceScale=2.0
r.Shadow.RadiusThreshold=0.02
r.DFDistanceScale=2.0
r.MinScreenRadiusForCSMDepth=0.01
r.HLOD.ForceDisableCastDynamicShadow=0
r.LightMaxDrawDistanceScale=2.0
r.MinScreenRadiusForLights=0.02
r.Decal.FadeScreenSizeMult=0.5

Textures loaded at highest resolution and distance:
r.MipMapLODBias=-1
r.Streaming.MipBias=0
r.Streaming.UseFixedPoolSize=0
r.Streaming.FullyLoadUsedTextures=1
r.Streaming.HLODStrategy=2
