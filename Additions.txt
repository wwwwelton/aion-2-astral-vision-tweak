
Optional tweaks to add to engine.ini

Copy and paste your selected options under all 4 main [brackets] to ensure they apply:
                                                    1 [/Script/Engine.Engine]
                                                    2 [ConsoleVariables]
                                                    3 [/Script/Engine.RendererSettings]
                                                    4 [SystemSettings]


-To enable FSR Frame Generation, use the options in "FSR Frame Generation.txt"
-To reduce stutters, use the options in "Reduce Stutters.txt"
-To optimize FSR, DLSS, and XESS, use the options in "FSR, DLSS, XESS.txt"

-Use the following Steam Launch Options -xgeshadercompile -nothreadtimeout


-Improve Lighting:

DisplayGamma=2.5
r.Color.Min=-0.0008
r.Color.Max=1.2
r.Color.Mid=0.65
r.Color.Grading=1


-Remove skill effects:

r.ParticleLightQuality=0
r.EmitterSpawnRateScale=0


-Reduce latency drastically:

r.FinishCurrentFrame=0
r.OneFrameThreadLag=0
framegrabber.framelatency=0
bSmoothFrameRate=0
t.AllowFrameRateSmoothing=0


Thank you.
