                                   4 [SystemSettings]

Reduces how far the game draws and keeps detail. Marked, these lines override the in-game sliders.
Mobs and players popping in closer is the expected side effect: leave it off if that bothers you.

View distance (players, mobs, objects, everything with a MaxDrawDistance):
r.ViewDistanceScale=0.4

Players and mobs (skeletal meshes):
r.SkeletalMeshLODBias=2
r.SkeletalMeshLODRadiusScale=0.5

Objects (static and Nanite meshes):
r.StaticMeshLODDistanceScale=2
r.Nanite.MaxPixelsPerEdge=2

Foliage and grass:
foliage.LODDistanceScale=0.5
foliage.CullDistanceScale=0.5
foliage.MinLOD=1
foliage.OverestimateLOD=0
grass.CullDistanceScale=0.5

Terrain:
r.LandscapeLODDistributionScale=0.75
r.LandscapeLOD0DistributionScale=0.75

Shadows:
r.Shadow.DistanceScale=0.6
r.Shadow.RadiusThreshold=0.06
r.DFDistanceScale=0.5
r.MinScreenRadiusForCSMDepth=0.02
r.HLOD.ForceDisableCastDynamicShadow=1

Lights and decals:
r.LightMaxDrawDistanceScale=0.5
r.MinScreenRadiusForLights=0.06
r.Decal.FadeScreenSizeMult=2
